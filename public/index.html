> SECTION_01

What I'm doing now

Building two companies at once, which I don't recommend and keep doing anyway.

LocalGhost.ai is a local-first, privacy-by-architecture AI platform. Everything runs on hardware you own, in code you can audit, with no cloud dependency. The cypherpunk thesis applied to personal AI, since a model that can't see your data can't sell your data. It's past Phase 1 and the box on my desk has captioned and indexed 30,408 photos and 2,573 videos without sending one of them anywhere.

Anchor Terminal grades the tools AI agents run on, from models and frameworks to data providers, scraping and payments. The things agents run on are where crypto exchanges were around 2016, so I'm running the playbook that built the CryptoCompare exchange benchmark again, this time with Quynh, who was the first CPO at CryptoCompare.

Writing the Hard Truths series alongside both. Posts on AI sycophancy, the skills pipeline, founder dynamics, and what happens to a civilisation that adds bureaucracy faster than it adds builders.

Romanian by birth, British by accident, diving and sailing around the world, working out where in the Mediterranean to live next, currently exploring Italy and Greece, with multiple autumns and springs already logged in Lagos, Portugal.

> SECTION_02

LocalGhost.ai. The only cloud is you.

Your AI, your data, your hardware. A small silent box on your desk that runs the whole stack locally, with no subscription, no telemetry and no backdoor. If LocalGhost the company disappears tomorrow, your box keeps working.

ghost.notedghost.framedghost.voicedghost.tallydghost.synthdghost.cuedghost.mistdghost.shadowdghost.secdghost.watchd
  • Fully open source, with every line of code auditable.
  • Architecturally blind, since the system prevents us from seeing your data.
  • Buy the hardware once from us, or build it yourself from the open BOM.
  • Daemons own the control flow and call local models the way another daemon would call Postgres.

> SECTION_03

Anchor Terminal. Grade them all. Anchor the one.

Agents are the buyers now, and most of them still pick tools by guessing. Anchor Terminal grades everything agents run on from public evidence, has a panel of agents review it, and publishes the arithmetic. We have our agents do the work so yours can just get the rankings and use the right tool for the right job.

> letme find "speech to text"
 
  • Every listing graded AA to F from public evidence, with the score arithmetic public and every deduction sourced.
  • Reviewed by a panel of agents, each on a different base model with a different thing to be annoyed about, one on security, one on cost, and so on.
  • Agent first, human second. Every page has a Markdown twin, and the day after we started counting, agents were making about 500 visits an hour, mostly for the .md files.
  • letme.dev picks the top-graded tool for a task. Calling it for you is next.
  • Rankings are never for sale. Audits are, for teams that want to know where agents get stuck on their product.
